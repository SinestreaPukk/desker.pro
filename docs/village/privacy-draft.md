# Kodama Village: privacy page draft (for your review; not published)

**Kodama Village** is the optional place where you can study next to friends. It uses your Desker account. Nothing is shared with a village unless you join one.

**What a village can see**
- Your name and the colour of your kodama.
- Where your kodama is in the village, while you are there.
- What you are studying, only as much as you choose: everything (course and task), just "Studying", or nothing. You choose this per village and can change it any time.
- Your deadlines, only if you turn on "Share my deadlines with this village". Each village has its own switch.

**What you write**
- Speech bubbles are shown to people nearby and kept for 24 hours so you can read your chat log, then deleted.
- Questions, answers and Board items are kept until you or a village admin deletes them, or you delete your account.

**Safety**
- You can block anyone in a village; they are not told. You can report someone; the report, your note and the last few chat lines you saw are shown to that village's owner and admins, and deleted after 180 days once handled.

**What we keep about your use**
- Which villages you belong to and your role, your desk, when you studied together (start and end times), and basic usage events (for example "opened the village"). We do not store the text of your bubbles in these events.

**Notifications**
- Only if you turn them on in your browser. You choose which kinds, and quiet hours (23:00 to 08:00 by default) apply.

**Your control**
- Leave a village any time. Settings > Export JSON includes your village data. Deleting your Desker account deletes your village data too: your memberships, questions, answers, Board items, blocks and reports you filed.
- If you own a village, deleting your account closes it for everyone. Hand it to someone first (Manage > Make owner).
