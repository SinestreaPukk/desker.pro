## How the art is made

`art/authoring/gen_sprites.py` and `gen_house.py` place palette letters on explicit grids and write `art/sprites/*.sprite.txt` and `art/props/house.sprite.txt`. Those text files are the source; nothing is generated by a model or resampled. `art/scene_build.py` composes the scene from a text tilemap plus props and runs the dusk and night remap tables.
