## How the art is made

`art/authoring/gen_sprites.py` and `gen_house.py` place palette letters on explicit grids and write `art/sprites/*.sprite.txt` and `art/props/house.sprite.txt`. Those text files are the source; nothing is generated by a model or resampled. `art/scene_build.py` draws the scene plane by plane (sky, peaks, forest ridge, far shore, lake and reflections, torii, near bank, foreground) with *roles* instead of colours, then three role-to-yuki16 tables colour it as night (master), morning and dusk. Props are bespoke code-drawn shapes, not tiles. `python3 art/scene_build.py --check` prints the role pairs that merge in a variant. `art/authoring/gen_avatar_scene.py` derives `av-scene` (the branchless perch pose) from `av-main`. `art/authoring/scene_thumbs.py` draws the composition thumbnails.


## Scene redo: composition choice

`design/screens/scene-thumbs.png` has three value-only thumbnails (96x40, yoru-2 / yoru-0 / yuki-3 / yuki-1).

| | Focal point | Depth planes | Eye path |
|---|---|---|---|
| A, peak centre, torii in the lake, avatar right | the avatar: the only bright mass against the dark lake, on the right third line | peaks, then lake with torii and far-shore house, then the bright bank | moon (upper left) down to the torii, across the lake to the house, round to the avatar |
| B, house large left | the house, which competes with the avatar | two planes, the house covers the middle | stalls at the house |
| C, low horizon, lake-heavy | none: the torii and the avatar stack on one vertical | lake fills the frame, mountains are a thin strip | straight up and down |

A wins: the clearest three-plane read, one focal point, and a zig-zag path. Light comes from the moon, upper left, so every lit face on every object faces upper-left.

## Scene redo: how the variants stay readable

Pixels are drawn once as roles. Morning is a low sun from the left (the moon becomes the sun), a pale lilac horizon and long cool shadows (`sn_s` is yuki-3). Dusk is a cold hada-1 / yoru-0 sky with a pink-lit snow face and rim. Stars map to the sky colour in the morning. The light pool maps to plain snow in the morning. If a variant stops reading, fix its column in `R` in `scene_build.py`, not the art.
